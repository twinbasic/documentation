# TBMANLIB_ConsoleComponents --- working notes

The contributed TBMANLIB_ConsoleComponents package, documented at
`docs/Reference/Contributed/TBMANLIB_ConsoleComponents/`. This file holds what its section decided
and what is known about the package beyond its pages; the rules for every contributed package are
in [WIP.Contributed.md](WIP.Contributed.md).

TBMANLIB_ConsoleComponents' console claims follow the source, because the samples harness cannot capture a console.

**Listed under Experimental Packages** (owner): TBMANLIB_ConsoleComponents cannot take a cConsole of the published TBMANLIB_ConsoleCore (it embeds a copy of it, the same package at 1.0.0.1, whose types the compiler treats as different; checked in a project made by the IDE's own import, in either order).
