# IDE Reference

The parts of the IDE that are described one control at a time: the menus, the title bar, the toolbar, the status bar, the commands and their keyboard shortcuts, and the Webpage pane.

- [**Menu bar**](Reference/Menu) --- the eleven menus along the top of the window, with every command and its shortcut.
- [**Title Bar**](Reference/TitleBar) --- the project's name, the **ADMIN** badge and the window's buttons.
- [**Toolbar**](Reference/Toolbar) --- the buttons under the menu bar, and the build configuration box.
- [**Status Bar**](Reference/StatusBar) --- the strip along the bottom of the window, which shows the state of the compiler services and the licence.
- [**Keyboard Shortcuts**](Reference/Keyboard-Shortcuts) --- every command of the IDE, with what it does and its default keys.
- [**Webpage**](Reference/Webpage) --- the pane that shows a web page inside the IDE.
