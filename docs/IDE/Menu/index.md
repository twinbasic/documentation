# Menu

The menu bar runs along the top of the IDE window, in the [title bar](TitleBar). Each menu has a page here, and each entry says what the item does, gives its key, and links to the chapter that explains it.

![The twinBASIC menu bar, with the window's buttons at the right-hand end.](Images/Menu.png){:width="611" height="25"}

- [**File**](Menu/File) --- opens, saves, exports and builds the project.
- [**Edit**](Menu/Edit) --- changes and searches text.
- [**View**](Menu/View) --- switches between code and designer, moves around the code, changes the zoom and shows the panels.
- [**Project**](Menu/Project) --- adds to the project, changes its references and settings, and opens its folders.
- [**Format**](Menu/Format) --- lines up and orders the controls selected in the form designer.
- [**Debug**](Menu/Debug) --- steps through a paused program, and holds the watches and breakpoints.
- [**Run**](Menu/Run) --- starts, pauses and ends a program.
- [**Tools**](Menu/Tools) --- the IDE Options, and the compiler.
- [**Add-Ins**](Menu/AddIns) --- the add-ins that are loaded.
- [**Window**](Menu/Window) --- panel layouts, keyboard shortcuts, themes and the language.
- [**Help**](Menu/Help) --- the About box, the licence, and the links to get help.

Press and release <kbd>Alt</kbd> to open the **File** menu. While a menu is open, <kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd> move through its items, <kbd>Left</kbd> and <kbd>Right</kbd> move to the next menu or in and out of a submenu, <kbd>Enter</kbd> runs the item, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the menu.

While the pointer is over an item, the [status bar](StatusBar#status) shows the name of its command. That is the name that a [keyboard shortcut group](../Customising/Keyboard-Shortcuts#the-text-of-a-group) uses.
