# Watches

When a project isn't open this will be empty.

![The WATCHES panel with no project open: a title bar holding only the drag grip and a close button, above an empty body.](Images/Watches.png){:width="300" height="76"}

Once you open a project it will other buttons available.

![The same panel with a project open, two more buttons now in its title bar: a plus for adding a watch, and a stack of lines with a small x for clearing the list.](Images/Watches_1.png){:width="300" height="76"}

If you wish to add a new watch click ![a thin grey plus sign](Images/Add.png) and type in your "Expression to watch"

![The panel after the plus is clicked, a white text box across the top of its body holding the grey placeholder text Expression to watch.](Images/Watches_2.png){:width="300" height="76"}

You can remove any with the Clear button ![four stacked lines with a small x at their lower right](Images/Clear.png)
