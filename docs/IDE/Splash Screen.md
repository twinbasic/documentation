# Splash Screen

![The twinBASIC splash window, its title bar reading Loading, please wait. The twinBASIC logo sits above a banner thanking the VIP Gold Supporters and a grid of six tiles: four sponsor logos and two placeholders inviting the reader to become a VIP Gold supporter. A twinBASIC Ltd copyright line closes the window.](Images/Splash_Screen.png)

The Splash Screen appears each time the IDE starts and closes automatically once loading finishes. Its title bar reads **Loading, please wait...** for as long as it is on screen.

Below the twinBASIC logo, a banner thanks the VIP Gold Supporters and a grid of tiles carries their logos. A tile with no sponsor reads **See your logo here!** and invites the reader to become one. A twinBASIC Ltd copyright line closes the window.

> [!NOTE]
> The splash screen shows no version number and no build date. Those are on the **About twinBASIC** dialog --- see [Help](Menu/Help), which also repeats the VIP Gold Supporters grid.
