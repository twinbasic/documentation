# TBMANLIB_ConsoleCore --- working notes

The contributed TBMANLIB_ConsoleCore package, documented at
`docs/Reference/Contributed/TBMANLIB_ConsoleCore/`. This file holds what its section decided and
what is known about the package beyond its pages; the rules for every contributed package are in
[WIP.Contributed.md](WIP.Contributed.md).

TBMANLIB_ConsoleCore's console claims are checked by its test cases, each on a console screen buffer of its own with its input written by `WriteConsoleInput`.

**Listed under Experimental Packages** (owner): TBMANLIB_ConsoleCore reads keys wrongly.
