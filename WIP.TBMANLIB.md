# TBMANLIB --- working notes

The contributed TBMANLIB package, documented at `docs/Reference/Contributed/TBMANLIB/`. This file
holds what its section decided and what is known about the package beyond its pages; the rules for
every contributed package are in [WIP.Contributed.md](WIP.Contributed.md).

**Listed under Experimental Packages** (owner): TBMANLIB 1.0.0.29 does not compile in a 64-bit build (TB5001 in cSubClass, where the window procedure is passed as a Long) and has many members that fail or give wrong results.
