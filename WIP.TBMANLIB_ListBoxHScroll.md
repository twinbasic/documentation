# TBMANLIB_ListBoxHScroll --- working notes

The contributed TBMANLIB_ListBoxHScroll package, documented at
`docs/Reference/Contributed/TBMANLIB_ListBoxHScroll/`. This file holds what its section decided and
what is known about the package beyond its pages; the rules for every contributed package are in
[WIP.Contributed.md](WIP.Contributed.md).

**Listed under Experimental Packages** (owner): TBMANLIB_ListBoxHScroll 1.0.0.1 moves the thumb 20 px per arrow and one page per track click while the list box scrolls its contents by its own step, so thumb and text disagree (confirmed by a second agent, owner).
